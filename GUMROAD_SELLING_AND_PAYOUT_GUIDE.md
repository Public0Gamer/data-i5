# 💰 Gumroad Selling & Automated License Delivery Guide
### Product: ClientHunter AI (Autonomous Lead & Social Scraper SaaS)

---

## 🎯 1. Gumroad Product Setup (Automated Access After Payment)

1. [Gumroad.com](https://gumroad.com) par free account banayein.
2. Click **Products** -> **New Product**:
   - **Type:** Digital Product
   - **Name:** `ClientHunter AI — Autonomous B2B Lead & Social Scraper SaaS (Web Access + License)`
   - **Price:** `$29` USD (~₹2,450 INR) ya `$49` USD (~₹4,100 INR).

3. **Description (Copy-Paste this high-converting copy):**
   ```text
   Stop paying $99/mo for Apollo or ZoomInfo!

   ClientHunter AI is an autonomous web-based client hunter that finds ready-to-buy local businesses in any niche & location across the globe.

   ✨ Features:
   - Zero API Costs: Free autonomous scraping engine.
   - Niche & City Targeting: Dental clinics, Real estate, Gyms, Agencies, Cafes, and more.
   - Complete Contact Matrix: Business Name, Phone Number, Website, Contact Email & Verified Instagram Handles.
   - 1-Click Instant CSV Export for cold outreach.
   - Web-Based Cloud Access: Run anywhere from your browser without installing anything!

   🔑 After purchase, you will receive instant web access link and your unique license activation key.
   ```

4. **License Keys Enable Karein:**
   - Product settings mein **"Generate unique license key per sale"** option ko **ON** karein.
   - Gumroad har buyer ko payment ke baad automatic ek unique license key generate karke receipt mein de dega!

5. **Content / Redirect URL:**
   - Content section mein apna live Web App URL daal dein (e.g. `https://client-hunter-ai.onrender.com` ya local package).
   - Buyer payment karega -> URL khulega -> apni Key daalega -> Tool activate!

---

## 🏦 2. Bank Payout Setup (Dollars to INR in Indian Bank)

1. Gumroad Dashboard -> **Settings** -> **Payouts**.
2. Country: **India** select karein.
3. Bank Transfer Details (via Stripe Connect) ya PayPal enter karein.
4. Gumroad automatically every Friday aapke dollar revenue ko INR mein convert karke direct bank account mein transfer karta hai.
